Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Minbari is an app that helps a reciter organize his readings. It was developed by Hussein Fouani and is given to readers directly by the app's owner, not through app stores. This page explains what the app keeps, where, and who can see it.

What the app keeps

  • Your account: your name and email address from the Google account you sign in with.
  • Your profile: the name, phone number and home town you enter.
  • Your schedule: readings, places, hosts and their numbers, people who asked for you, notes, recurring readings, reminders and your reading hours. You enter all of this yourself.
  • Your subscription: the fee, the months paid as the owner records them, when your phone last reached the server, the app version, how many times the account moved to another phone, and a random number that identifies this installation of the app (so the account works on one phone at a time).

The app does not keep your location and keeps no voice recordings.

Where it is kept

  • On your phone: in the app's database.
  • In the cloud: a copy in Google Firebase, on servers in Europe, under your account only.
  • Phone backup: if Google backup is on for your phone, the app's data is included in it.

Data travels encrypted between the phone and the server.

Who can see it

  • Only you can see your schedule, readings and notes. The server's rules stop every other account from reading them, the owner's included.
  • The app's owner sees only your card: name, phone number, home town, app version and last connection, plus your subscription and payments. He needs these to manage subscriptions.
  • Minbari sells no data, shows no ads, and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

Permissions

  • Microphone: for voice commands only: when you press «تكلّم» (Speak), or while the driving screen is open if you chose «Listen on the driving screen». Speech is turned into text by your phone's speech-recognition service (usually Google's), which may process the audio on its servers under its own policy. While listening for «يا منبري», the phone's own on-device recognition is used where the phone has it, so that audio stays on the phone. Minbari keeps no audio.
  • Calendar: only when you copy your readings to the phone's calendar.
  • Phone calls: only on the driving screen, when you answer «نعم» to «هل أتصل الآن؟» (Shall I call now?): the app asks for this permission the first time and then places the call to that host's number without the dialler's tap. If you refuse, the dialler opens as before. The app does not read your call log.
  • Notifications and alarms: for the reminders you choose; each can be turned off. In driving mode a «Driving mode is on» notification stays while the app reads reminders aloud.
  • Contacts: when you pick a contact, only the name and number you picked reach the app. If you turn on «Copy hosts to the phone's contacts», it writes your hosts into the phone's contacts and keeps them current, and reads no others; turning it off removes what it wrote.
  • Clipboard: only when you tap paste beside a phone number: the app reads the copied text once to take the number from it.
  • Physical activity: only if you turn on «Open driving mode automatically»: through Google services, the phone tells the app when you get into or out of a car. Nothing about it is kept or sent.

Other services

  • Google Firebase and Google Sign-In: for the account, the cloud copy and update delivery.
  • OpenStreetMap: when you open the map to place a pin, map images load from its servers, which see your IP address and the area shown. The place name you typed (nothing else) is sent to it to search in Lebanon.
  • Google Maps short links: when you share a message with a short location link into Minbari, the app asks Google's servers where the link leads. Only the link is sent.
  • Your phone's text-to-speech: spoken replies and reminders use the voice engine on your phone (usually Google's), which may turn the text into speech on its servers under its own policy.
  • Your phone's apps: when you tap call, WhatsApp or open the map, that app opens and its own policy applies.

Log of sentences not understood

If you agree to it in Settings, the app keeps the text of sentences it did not understand (never audio) for 30 days, on your phone (and in its Google backup, if that is on), to improve. It is sent to no one unless you share it yourself, and you can turn it off and clear it at any time.

Deleting

  • One item: what you delete goes to «Deleted items», where it can be restored for 90 days.
  • The whole account: in Account → Delete my account. You have 30 days to restore it; after that your data and account are erased from the cloud for good.
  • From the phone: uninstalling the app erases its copy on the phone.

The record of subscription payments stays with the owner, as a financial record.

Changes to this policy

If it changes, the new version arrives with an app update and the «Last updated» date above changes.

Contact

For any question about your data, contact the developer at the number in «About Minbari».
